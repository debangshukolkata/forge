How enrolment works

Four short steps, and most families finish them in one evening.

  1. Pick a course

    Choose a course and a start date from the list above.

  2. Book a trial

    Join a free twenty-minute taster call with the teacher.

  3. Confirm and pay

    Reserve the seat by card or in two instalments.

  4. Get your welcome pack

    We send the class link, the materials list and a first task.
