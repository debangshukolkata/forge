How a day goes

No timetable to decode. Come for one part or stay for all four.

  1. 1
    6:30 am

    Arrive

    Tea in the garden room while the floors warm. Leave your shoes and your inbox at the door.

  2. 2
    7:00 am

    Move

    An hour of slow flow, with options for every body and every kind of stiff Tuesday.

  3. 3
    8:15 am

    Rest

    Twenty minutes of guided stillness under blankets. Most people fall asleep. That is allowed.

  4. 4
    9:00 am

    Eat

    Porridge, stewed fruit and good coffee at the long table, then back out into the day.
