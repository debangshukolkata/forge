Our story

It began with one borrowed oven

In 2014 Marian Holt cooked a Sunday dinner for six neighbours who had run out of money before the end of the month. Word travelled. Within a year there were forty at her table and a queue at her door.

Today the Trust is two kitchens, thirty-eight allotments and three hundred volunteers. The table is still the point: nobody is handed a bag and sent away.
