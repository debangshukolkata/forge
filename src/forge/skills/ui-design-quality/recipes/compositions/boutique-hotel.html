The stay

Small things done well

Breakfast

Warm bread, local honey and eggs from the farm up the hill, served from 8 to 11 on the terrace.

Supper

One short menu each night, built around the morning's catch. Please reserve by noon.

The water

Steps lead to a cove of smooth stones. Towels, masks and kayaks are kept by the gate.

The quiet

No television in the rooms and no music by the pool. Books and board games fill the library.
