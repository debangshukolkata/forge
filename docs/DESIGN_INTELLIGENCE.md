# Design Intelligence Engine: the discussion of 2026-10-15

Written down so that the next sessions start from it. The user's brief: discover excellent design, understand why it works, turn that into an explicit blueprint, implement it, and validate the rendered result,
so that pages look intentionally designed for their brand and not like variations of one template. The work items are DIE1 to DIE7 in TODO.md section 0d.

## What exists already
`design_inspire` (public pages in an isolated browser, measured colours, fonts, sizes and radii, a DESIGN.md draft), DESIGN.md with a concept and a signature move and a linter, three candidates with a novelty rule and
an independent critic (a separate role since D-409), the audit with hygiene checks (D-419), the design skill, compositions and screens. Gaps: discovery only comes late (step 3 of the ladder), no knowledge base,
the blueprint has no imagery, motion or reason-for-each-decision sections, and design and code are not separate components.

## Agreed with the user, and where I differ
- Agreed: discovery first, a structured blueprint, a searchable knowledge base, design separate from code, mandatory screenshot iteration.
- Not crawled: Dribbble and Behance (terms forbid scraping, login and JavaScript, no open API). References come from live public sites, from the user, and from our own base.
- Measure, then interpret: a vision model reads feel well and spacing badly; the browser gives the real computed styles.
- The knowledge base keeps principles, measurements, source links and a small local thumbnail, not copies of third-party assets.
- Distinctiveness is advice from a cross-family critic and the user's eye; only measurable things are gates (pictures, alignment, fonts, card density, distance from our defaults and from earlier projects).
- unDraw is not used automatically (D-420).
