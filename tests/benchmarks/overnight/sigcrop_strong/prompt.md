In this folder there is a photo of a handwritten medical prescription: prescription.jpg (a fictional sample). Write a Python command-line script extract_signature.py that finds the doctor's signature in such a photo and saves it as a cropped PNG with a transparent background, the ink keeping its original colour: `python extract_signature.py prescription.jpg signature.png`.

The signature is the pen scrawl at the bottom of the page, after the prescription text. It is not the printed letterhead at the top, not the handwritten patient name or date, and not the handwritten medicine lines. Do not use machine-learning models; Pillow and NumPy (and scikit-image or OpenCV only if you install them into this project's own environment) are fine. The script must work on other photos of the same kind, not only this one: the signature can be somewhat larger, smaller or in a slightly different place.

Look at the photo yourself first. Run your script on it, view the result, and keep improving until the output contains only the signature, with the paper and the folds gone. Add a small pytest test that would FAIL on a wrong crop (a minimum size, the ink inside it), not only one that checks the output is smaller than the input. Do not print or copy any patient information into your replies or files.
