Create three throwaway designs of the home screen for a blood donation app called Pulse, for people who donate or want to start. The screen shows the next donation they can book, nearby donation centres, and how many lives their donations have helped. The three designs must look clearly different from each other (different mood, colours, type and layout), because the team will pick one. Put each in its own folder, designs/design-a, designs/design-b and designs/design-c, each with an index.html and its own style.css (plain HTML and CSS, JavaScript only where it adds something). Each must work on a phone. Look at each one in a browser at desktop and phone width before you finish. Do not ask me questions; decide yourself.
